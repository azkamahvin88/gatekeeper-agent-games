## Draft thread (English, 7 post)

1/
I let AI agents move my money every single day. Mints, swaps, stakes — all executed by agents. Here's the uncomfortable truth: I can't actually PROVE what they did. 🧵 #AgentGames

2/
On-chain, I can see a transaction happened. What I can't see: WHY the agent did it. Did it follow my rules? Was it tricked? Or just lucky? The tx is a receipt. The reasoning is invisible.

3/
So my rule is simple: simulate before you send. Every mint and swap gets a preview first — expected gain, no stray approvals, gas in range. Wrong simulation = nothing moves. But that check is mine alone. Nobody else can verify it.

4/
Now scale it up. Agents approving payments. Agents trading. Agents signing contracts. "Trust me" doesn't scale — one bad decision is real money gone, and a receipt doesn't prove the decision was right.

5/
This is the problem zero-knowledge proofs solve for agents: prove the decision followed the rules, without revealing private data. Verify, don't trust.

6/
That's what @zkVerify is testing in Agent Games. In Proof Gatekeeper, agents judge proof-backed payments — and every decision ships with a platform-verified proof. The result isn't a claim. It's a public record anyone can check.

7/
